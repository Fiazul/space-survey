# Astryx test-2026-10-09

Android version `0.15.0-dev.20261009.1` (code `2026100901`). Signed arm64 debug
APK for sideload testing, package `com.fiazul.astryx`.

- Add the Milky Way galactic core and Sagittarius A* inspection destinations,
  Kerr lensing, animated accretion flow, hotspots and flares.
- Add black-hole flight gravity, orbital telemetry, plunge cues and camera shake.
- Force inward capture inside **0.6 AU**, including the reported 0.585 AU approach.
  Incoming speed, outward thrust and developer NODEATH cannot escape the region.
  The HUD shows **CORE CAPTURE — NO ESCAPE**; Humano starts at the horizon.
  This boundary is an intentional gameplay rule; ordinary physics applies outside it.
- Add Humano: a cubic-mist descent and loading transition to a third-person
  dinosaur playing a human endless runner. Jump with Space, Up, click/tap or the
  Jump button; exit returns to the ship at 2 AU, preserving hull and progress.
- Add a TELEPORT search entry for Humano and keep Sol's astronaut out of the core.
- Update Sovereign with matte armor, smaller bridge windows, radiator banks,
  coolant channels and detailed engine collars, plus editable Blender source.

## Bug record

**BH-001 (fixed):** The prior developer NODEATH bypass let the ship cross
Sagittarius A* without entering Humano. The first horizon-only fix was reverted;
this release includes the subsequent 0.6 AU forced-capture behavior requested
from the gameplay screenshot. The direct Humano preview also works with NODEATH.
See [the bug record](https://github.com/Fiazul/space-survey/blob/test-2026-10-09/docs/KNOWN-BUGS.md)
and [GitHub issue #3](https://github.com/Fiazul/space-survey/issues/3).

## Remaining known bug

**BH-002 (open):** In the software Vulkan Mobile close-core preview, the accretion
layer can obscure the foreground ship. Capture and Humano still work; physical
Android impact is unverified. See [issue #4](https://github.com/Fiazul/space-survey/issues/4).
